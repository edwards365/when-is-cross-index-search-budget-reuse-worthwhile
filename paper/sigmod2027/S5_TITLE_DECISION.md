# S5 Title Decision

## Selected title

**Auditing Search-Budget Portability Across Graph-ANNS Rebuilds: [Experiments & Analysis]**

## Decision

Retain the W6 title. It names the experimental object (search-budget portability), the intervention (graph rebuilds), and the paper's primary contribution (auditing). It remains accurate after S4: fresh-query evidence strengthens a conditional deployable bridge for Faiss HNSW but does not make recovery universal across implementations. The title also uses SIGMOD 2027's mandatory E&A suffix exactly.

## Rejected alternatives

- **When Search Budgets Do Not Transfer Across Graph-ANNS Rebuilds** is memorable but too categorical because S4 shows a certified one-rung bridge in the registered Faiss family.
- **Auditing and Recovering Search-Budget Portability** overstates the cross-implementation recovery result; hnswlib has no cross-dataset economic bridge.
- **ICBA: Auditing Search-Budget Portability ...** makes an acronym, rather than the phenomenon and evidence, carry the title.
- **Search-Budget Portability ...: Risks, Certification, and Recovery** is accurate but longer and produces an awkward double-colon before the mandatory E&A suffix.

The short running title remains `Auditing Search-Budget Portability`.
