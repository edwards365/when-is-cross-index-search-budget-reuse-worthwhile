# S5 Title Decision

## Selected title

**Auditing Search-Budget Portability Across Graph-ANNS Rebuilds: [Experiments & Analysis]**

## Decision

Retain the title. It names the experimental object (search-budget portability), the intervention (graph rebuilds), and the paper's organizing contribution (auditing). The stronger evidence now includes target-certified recovery on Faiss at 100K scale and hnswlib at Deep1M scale, but each result remains conditional on a registered candidate family, target query roles, and build set. Keeping recovery in the subtitle-level narrative rather than the title avoids turning those scoped results into a universal recovery claim. The title also uses SIGMOD 2027's mandatory E&A suffix exactly.

## Rejected alternatives

- **When Search Budgets Do Not Transfer Across Graph-ANNS Rebuilds** is memorable but too categorical because S4 shows a certified one-rung bridge in the registered Faiss family.
- **Auditing and Recovering Search-Budget Portability** makes recovery appear universal, whereas the evidence identifies both successful and endpoint-clipped implementation--grid regimes.
- **ICBA: Auditing Search-Budget Portability ...** makes an acronym, rather than the phenomenon and evidence, carry the title.
- **Search-Budget Portability ...: Risks, Certification, and Recovery** is accurate but longer and produces an awkward double-colon before the mandatory E&A suffix.

The short running title remains `Auditing Search-Budget Portability`.
