\section{Related Work and Positioning}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Graph construction, evaluation, and maintenance.}
HNSW combines hierarchical navigation with graph search, while DiskANN introduces Vamana as part of a system for large-scale search~\cite{malkov2020hnsw,subramanya2019diskann}. Azizi et al.~\cite{azizi2025evaluation} compare graph-search methods and construction paradigms across datasets and scales. Elliott and Clark~\cite{elliott2024ordering} show that insertion order and intrinsic dimensionality affect HNSW recall. These studies establish that the constructed graph matters to search quality. We examine a different experimental unit: a budget policy transferred from a source build to a target build, with a matched reference used to separate target difficulty from transfer loss. FreshDiskANN~\cite{singh2021freshdiskann} supports insertions and deletions without repeatedly reconstructing the entire index. Such maintenance mechanisms motivate distinguishing graph-only rebuilding from refresh-plus-rebuild; the latter is the setting of our primary TCP replay.

\paragraph{Adaptive work allocation and declarative recall.}
LAET predicts query-specific termination conditions using both static and intermediate-search features~\cite{li2020laet}. DARTH instead predicts achieved recall during execution and adapts prediction intervals to terminate HNSW or IVF searches at a declarative target~\cite{chatzakis2025darth}. ANNiE estimates the work required to reach a recall target from graph-derived features; its ANNiE-S search uses those estimates directly~\cite{wang2026annie}. Ada-ef models query-to-dataset similarity distributions to choose an HNSW exploration parameter~\cite{zhang2026adaef}. It also studies incremental updates to its statistics and estimation table, including comparisons with stale and fully recomputed variants after insertions or deletions. Our question concerns the validity and cost of carrying a decision between builds. It applies to adaptive policies as well as fixed budgets, but comparisons must retain each policy's permitted signals and update procedure. The frozen DARTH and Ada-ef bridges are evidence about those integrations, rather than a common ranking of all published methods.

\paragraph{Quality guarantees and their target quantities.}
ConANN applies conformal risk control to IVF search, controlling expected false-negative rate while adapting the number of probed clusters~\cite{horchidan2025conann}. Its objective is the expected fraction of missed neighbors, whereas our principal event is whether a query falls below a recall threshold. ANNiE's optional recall-target guarantee addresses the fraction of queries reaching the requested recall using quantile-loss minimization~\cite{wang2026annie}. The DARTH+ preprint also introduces optional probabilistic quality guarantees for HNSW and IVF~\cite{chatzakis2026darthplus}. We study the build and policy covered by such an assessment, the information reused or reacquired, and the resulting behavior after rebuilding. The risk quantities remain explicit in comparisons.

\paragraph{Calibration, selection, and the role of ICBA.}
Conformal risk control bounds an expected monotone loss under its calibration assumptions~\cite{angelopoulos2024crc}. Learn then Test formulates risk-controlled parameter selection as multiple hypothesis testing~\cite{angelopoulos2021ltt}. These are statistical foundations for auditing a search decision, rather than guarantees that automatically survive a changed execution environment. ConANN explicitly aligns calibration with index construction and assumes exchangeable query data~\cite{horchidan2025conann}. In our setting, rebuilding can change the loss induced by the search even when the query population is retained. ICBA makes the target build, failure event, candidate selection, and fallback rule explicit. TCP supplies a concrete query-profile reuse and recovery case within that audit. This separation lets us ask whether target information restores useful search efficiency without conflating empirical recovery, a pipeline-level certificate, and net lifecycle savings.
