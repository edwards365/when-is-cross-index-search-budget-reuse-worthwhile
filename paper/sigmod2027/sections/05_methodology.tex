\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:methodology}

\paragraph{Primary refresh experiment.}
The primary workload has 100,000 base vectors per dataset: 128-dimensional SIFT-100K with Euclidean distance and 768-dimensional Arxiv-Nomic-100K with normalized inner product. A seed-991 membership change replaces 5\% of the original collection with previously excluded vectors, preserving cardinality. Exact neighbors are computed against the refreshed snapshot. We reuse ten registered old-snapshot and ten refreshed-snapshot indexes per dataset. The native replay runs the official DARTH Faiss-HNSW fork, with $M=16$, construction $ef=100$, one search thread, $k=10$, and the seven-action grid in Equation~\eqref{eq:tcp-grid}. Thus the primary recovery experiment is a Faiss-HNSW replay; the hnswlib scale evidence is a separate block.

For each target, 500 queries select a policy, a disjoint 500 certify it, and a disjoint 1,000 evaluate it. The same role IDs are reused across builds. Content-hash checks separate these roles from base vectors, insertion reserves, and historical query roles. The source-history profiles in Section~\ref{sec:auditor} exist for these same queries on the old builds. This is a recurring-profile workload after refresh plus rebuilding; it isolates neither a graph-only causal effect nor generalization to unprofiled queries.

\paragraph{Metrics and decisions.}
We report empirical $\rec@10<0.95$ risk, mean distance evaluations, query-pooled p95 and p99 distance evaluations, candidate acceptance, and actual fallback. Relative search gain is
\begin{equation}
 g(\pi)=1-\frac{\bar c_\pi}{\bar c_{\rm end}},
 \label{eq:gain}
\end{equation}
where $\bar c_{\rm end}$ is the fixed-$ef=200$ mean on the same evaluation queries and target builds. Fallback executions remain in all denominators. Candidate and endpoint checks use the individual one-sided 95\% CP bounds specified in Section~\ref{sec:tcp-recalibration}; the empirical decision and joint confidence accounting are reported separately.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and robustness.}
The registered nested bootstrap draws target builds with replacement, then draws paired policy/reference queries within each selected build (5,000 replicates, seed 991). Each replicate computes a ratio of pooled means. Intervals are percentile summaries of this procedure. Shared query IDs across builds introduce crossed dependence that independent within-build resampling does not fully preserve; the reported intervals retain that limitation. Leave-one-target-out (LOTO) and deletion of the largest relative-gain build provide direct sensitivity checks over the ten fixed targets. Observed p95/p99 reductions are descriptive comparisons, not hypothesis-test noninferiority certificates. NDC is the common search-work measure; none of these gains establishes an equal wall-clock speedup.

\paragraph{Evidence order.}
RQ1 and RQ4 establish direct-reuse behavior and its audit decision. RQ5--RQ8 examine recovery, tails, build sensitivity, and amortization. RQ2--RQ3 then test the scope of the finding through external-method, graph-family, and scale evidence. RQ9 closes the numerical and replay provenance. Each block keeps its native action, reference, and query-risk definition.
