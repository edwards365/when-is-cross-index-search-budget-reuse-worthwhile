\section{Budget Portability on Rebuilt Graphs}
\label{sec:portability}
\sectionaim{Establish the phenomenon before discussing recovery.}

Table~\ref{tab:primary} shows the primary contrast. Direct source TCP pool reuse has empirical target failure of \WZeroSiftSourceRisk\% on SIFT and \WZeroArxivSourceRisk\% on Arxiv. Both exceed the 5\% target, despite reducing mean distance counts relative to the fixed endpoint. Thus, in this replay, low search work and target recall compliance are different outcomes.

The target-selection lane reduces the failure rates to \WZeroSiftTargetRisk\% and \WZeroArxivTargetRisk\%. Its mean search savings remain \WZeroSiftTargetGain\% and \WZeroArxivTargetGain\%. This contrast motivates the recovery analysis: target information changes the operating point, but its value must include acquisition costs and the validity of the resulting decision rule.
