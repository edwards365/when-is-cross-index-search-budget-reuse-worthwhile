\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Search-budget reuse across graph rebuilds is a measurable transfer decision. The frozen evidence shows target failures under direct source reuse and empirical recovery from target recalibration in the query-profile setting. ICBA connects those observations to explicit risk events, independent query roles, and lifecycle accounting. The resulting lesson is to evaluate portability and recovery together: retained search efficiency, statistical assurance, and the cost of acquiring target information describe different parts of the same operational decision.
