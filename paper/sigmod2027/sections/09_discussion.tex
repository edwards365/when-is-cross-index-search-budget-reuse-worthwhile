\section{Reproducibility and Operational Implications}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{RQ9: Can the Numbers and Decisions Be Replayed?}
\textbf{Question.} Can an evaluator trace the reported numbers to the inputs and decision rules?
\textbf{Protocol.} The evidence package binds input hashes, role IDs, analysis scripts, per-build tables, and decision manifests to a frozen numerical ledger. Its checker selects unique CSV/JSON rows, verifies risk/gain aggregation, and regenerates the LaTeX value macros. The portable interface separates a lightweight smoke, table reconstruction, an external-asset preflight, and guarded full replay.
\textbf{Observation.} The existing portability audit preserved hashes of all 80 pre-audit scientific files, passed a neutral-path smoke and 28 focused tests, and replayed the DARTH, Vamana, and Deep1M analyses from sealed inputs through explicit root arguments. The manuscript's 57 frozen values and its additional tail/sensitivity macros are checked against committed summaries; the tables themselves are typeset from those macros.
\textbf{Conclusion.} The package supports numerical traceability and tested replay interfaces. Recreating raw searches requires the separately provisioned vectors, truth, indexes, and native binaries identified by the checksum ledger. These reference-server checks are distinct from independent replication on a new installation.

\paragraph{Evidence and scope.}
The strongest recovery evidence is the two-dataset, ten-target recurring-profile experiment. The extension blocks answer narrower questions under their own reference policies. The theory separates observation-limited decisions, fixed-target query certificates, and build-marginal label coverage; the replay does not measure full-observation total variation or instantiate all conditions of the build-coverage result. These boundaries explain why empirical recovery, selected-policy confidence, and economic value are reported as separate outcomes.

\paragraph{Index-operation decision.}
An operator should version a budget policy together with its index snapshot, search implementation, query-profile population, and calibration record. A changed build triggers a concrete decision: audit reuse, acquire target evidence for recalibration, or retain the endpoint. Rejection can reflect inadequate certification power rather than actual unsafety. Conversely, an efficient policy that fails the registered event should not be hidden by reporting only its post-fallback average. The evidence favors selective recalibration when the profiled workload recurs long enough to repay acquisition, with a fresh cost and risk check for a changed workload.

\paragraph{Data and software availability.}
The reproducibility materials comprise protocol manifests, input checksum lists, analysis and table-generation code, compact result tables, and replay instructions. Large external vectors and pre-existing indexes are provisioned separately. The package distinguishes the lightweight numerical check from raw-query replay and identifies which inputs each requires.
