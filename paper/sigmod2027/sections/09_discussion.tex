\section{Reproducibility and Interpretation}
\label{sec:discussion}
\sectionaim{State the consequences of the evidence boundaries once, at the level where they change the conclusion.}

\paragraph{Evidence lineage.}
The numerical exhibits are generated from a frozen ledger linked to committed summary tables, per-build records, analysis code, and decision manifests. This writing stage does not regenerate raw query responses. The final artifact description will distinguish numerical consistency checks, replay reproducibility, and independent statistical validation.

\paragraph{Deployment population.}
The primary TCP result concerns queries with corresponding old-build profiles. Target-role separation does not make those source-side query profiles free or available for an unseen workload. This population definition is necessary both for a deployment claim and for amortization.

\paragraph{Theory and confidence.}
The theoretical component must state its observation model and prove its premises at the claimed level. An empirical classifier or a plug-in discrepancy does not automatically provide the upper bound required by an information-theoretic result. Likewise, separate single-action confidence bounds do not automatically certify a data-dependent candidate/fallback decision.

\paragraph{Implication for index operations.}
Budget policies should be versioned with the build and workload on which they were assessed. A rebuild can trigger a targeted audit without requiring a universal prescription to retrain every policy. The operator needs three separate answers: the new risk, the cost of a valid decision, and the horizon over which that decision is useful.
