\paragraph{Registered method bridges.}
The DARTH bridge records \WZeroDarthSiftRisk\% and \WZeroDarthArxivRisk\% target failure on SIFT and Arxiv, respectively. The AdaEF bridge on Arxiv records \WZeroAdaefArxivRisk\%. These observations belong to the specific registered adapters and threshold settings. The final comparison will state what was retained from the original method and what changed for the transfer test.

\paragraph{Vamana.}
The aligned post-hoc audit reports \WZeroVamanaSiftRisk\% and \WZeroVamanaArxivRisk\% risk for SIFT and Arxiv, using six source builds, six distinct target builds, 36 source--target pairs, and 750 queries. This evidence adds a graph-family case. Its post-hoc status and source-qualification rule remain visible rather than being collapsed into the primary replay table.

\paragraph{Deep1M.}
The scale block contains \WZeroDeepBuilds\ builds, \WZeroDeepQueries\ queries, and \WZeroDeepPairs\ directed pairs. Transport risk is \WZeroDeepRisk\%, while the reference risk is \WZeroDeepReferenceRisk\%. Their frozen difference is \WZeroDeepIncrement\ percentage points, with reported interval [\WZeroDeepIncrementLow, \WZeroDeepIncrementHigh]. The reference risk is already above 5\%; the difference measures an additional transfer penalty, not failure of an independently established safe reference.
