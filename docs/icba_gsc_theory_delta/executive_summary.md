# GSC theory-delta executive summary

**Decision:** `GSC_THEORY_VALID_CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_BRIDGE_REQUIRED`.

The adaptive-generation theorem is valid by conditioning on proposal/validation data and using an independent certification sample after freezing the final candidate family. This is a classical sample-splitting application, not a new adaptive-inference theorem.

The new stability object is useful but limited. (D_C), (D_Z), endpoint disagreement, rank reversal, raw-`ef` response, and expansion-prefix behavior are distinct. No general structure-to-risk or cost-to-risk implication exists. The only unconditional transfer relation is the event-disagreement bound (r(G',e)\le r(G,e)+P(Z_G\ne Z_{G'})), which is an identity/restricted proposition unless the disagreement term is independently estimated.

The positive-net-benefit rule and break-even expression are valid cost accounting. They do not turn oracle headroom or budget-proxy improvement into deployment gain. A candidate generator is not proven to produce a good candidate, and the latest CIBS closure supplies no two-dataset CI-supported joint-feasible baseline action.

Directed prior-art review found no single work satisfying the seven-condition direct-GSC prior test. Nevertheless, graph construction, ANN budget control, conformal risk control, active testing, SafeBAI, and algorithm portfolios cover the major components. The maximum defensible novelty is a narrow Graph-ANNS combination, pending experiment evidence.

GSC experiments may continue under a frozen protocol, without reading final evaluation during theory work. Any paper must keep fixed-target, registered-family claims separate from held-out-build and open-world claims.
