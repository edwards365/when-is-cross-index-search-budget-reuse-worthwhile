# GSC novelty audit

## Three-layer positioning

### Classical tools — not novel claims

Clopper–Pearson, Bonferroni/FWER, Learn-Then-Test, RCPS/CRC, Hoeffding/DKW/Bernoulli-KL, SafeBAI, Track-and-Stop, successive halving, CVaR, finite-class ERM, and break-even accounting are prior tools.

### Potential new combination

The plausible delta is the explicit Generate–Stabilize–Certify pipeline: graph construction is treated as a controllable hidden-environment channel; response dispersion and endpoint disagreement are measured separately; the final build×raw-`ef` family is frozen; and independent fixed-target certification is connected to total service cost and fallback. This is at most `NEW_COMBINATION_OF_CLASSICAL_RESULTS` until independently evaluated.

### Potential Graph-ANNS-specific method result

O2–O7 could support a systems-method contribution only if they produce a candidate outside the ordinary random-build Pareto set, pass real recall and tail gates, survive independent portfolios, and have finite service break-even. The theory agent must not pre-label that outcome.

## Direct-prior caveat

No seven-condition direct prior was found in the checked corpus, but the directed search is finite and QBAT/any unverified recent work can change the method verdict. Therefore no “first” claim is authorized.

## Current highest defensible statement

GSC is a conditional Graph-ANNS design-and-certification framework that combines adaptive candidate generation with independent fixed-target risk certification. Its structural-to-budget relation is an empirical bridge unless the restricted disagreement assumptions are independently verified.
