# Theory conflict resolution

This lock applies later semantic audits over earlier theory drafts. No statement below may be reversed by wording changes in the paper.

## Mandatory downgrades

1. Raw fixed-`ef` recall is not assumed monotone query by query.
2. `efSearch` is neither an expansion count nor an NDC/time cap.
3. DiskANN3/Vamana-style `l_value` is not numerically equivalent to HNSW `efSearch`.
4. Edge overlap does not generally imply budget-response stability.
5. Graph similarity does not imply endpoint-state agreement.
6. No general structure-to-budget positive bridge has been proved; existing trace bridges are restricted or empirical.
7. Query bootstrap quantifies uncertainty under a registered build family; it is not build bootstrap and not an unseen-build certificate.
8. Fixed-target certification does not imply open-world safety or safety after the next rebuild.
9. Per-query source or target Oracles are non-deployable upper bounds.
10. Mean-cost improvement does not imply p95 or p99 improvement.
11. Target evidence may certify safety but cannot guarantee positive net value.
12. Multi-lane complementarity does not imply a truth-free deployable trigger.
13. Safe selection cannot create a jointly safe, low-cost action absent from the frozen candidate pool.
14. Fixed-sequence or nested-DKW certification is prohibited for raw fixed-`ef` actions unless population-level pointwise nesting is proved independently; final ICBA uses Bonferroni/simultaneous control or certifies one independently selected action.
15. A nominal maximum-budget action is not a safe fallback without an independent certificate under the same endpoint-aware event; otherwise output abstention.

## Invalidated or deleted claims

- “first stable Graph-ANNS,” “first safe Graph-ANNS,” and “first rebuild-portable ANNS”;
- a new general Le Cam, KL, SafeBAI, LTT, RCPS, Hoeffding, or Clopper–Pearson theorem;
- general open-world positive recovery;
- equality of HNSW and Vamana raw budgets or costs;
- a universal positive conservative-cost tax across all Graph-ANNS;
- deployment value or state-of-the-art performance of any attempted recovery algorithm;
- safety inferred from an Oracle, observed sample nesting, edge similarity, or the maximum registered action alone.

## Surviving restricted results

- the hidden-environment lower bound as a Graph-ANNS loss instantiation of classical testing;
- endpoint lower bounds and risk decomposition;
- fixed-target finite-action screening and independent certification;
- the five recovery obstructions plus sufficient finite-class protocol;
- classical sample-complexity and break-even corollaries;
- additive rescue identities and reverse-pair symmetry as appendix propositions;
- data × operator interaction as an empirical estimand, not a universal law.
