# Final claim registry

## Claims permitted in the abstract

1. Across three registered Graph-ANNS implementations and two datasets, independent rebuilds materially change query-level safe budget requirements.
2. Source-to-target budget transport violations are positive in all six registered data × operator cells.
3. The phenomenon spans HNSW and Vamana-style construction under implementation-specific native action semantics.
4. Conservative-cost consequences are operator dependent: statistically resolved for the two registered HNSW implementations and unresolved for the registered Vamana-style implementation.
5. When build environments with conflicting useful actions are observationally indistinguishable, classical testing implies an unavoidable trade-off among unsafe under-budgeting, conservative computation, fallback, and abstention.
6. Fixed-target recovery can be certified for a finite frozen action family under endpoint feasibility, identifiability, positive margin, independent target evidence, and a valid fallback or abstention.

## Claims permitted in the introduction

- Build configuration is a hidden conditioning variable for query-budget decisions.
- Rebuild portability has not been adequately captured by single-index query adaptation alone.
- The registered evidence rules out a one-library or one-construction-paradigm anecdote.
- ICBA supplies a theory-guided audit interface for diagnosing portability and recovery conditions.
- The failed method families identify distinct missing conditions rather than merely reporting unsuccessful engineering.

## Claims restricted to results or discussion

- Exact variation rates and transport effects are conditional on registered grids, builds, query laws, and implementations.
- HNSW conservative-cost tax is positive under the registered NDC semantics; the Vamana-style estimate is near 1% and its interval crosses zero.
- Fixed-target certification may be safe while economically useless.
- Existing observable signals, stable-build surrogates, and rescue triggers did not close a deployable method loop.
- The legacy 123-item baseline is only conditionally reproducible: 111 matched, eight content hashes differed, and four tracked-list entries were unavailable `__pycache__` artifacts.

## Permanently prohibited claims

- all Graph-ANNS systems, all datasets, all Vamana implementations, all future rebuilds, or all query distributions behave identically;
- an open-world safety or positive-recovery guarantee;
- a new general Le Cam, KL, SafeBAI, LTT, RCPS, Hoeffding, or Clopper–Pearson theorem;
- numerical equivalence of hnswlib `efSearch`, Faiss `efSearch`, and DiskANN3 `l_value`;
- a universal conservative-cost tax;
- a deployable recovery algorithm, production QPS improvement, or SOTA retrieval performance;
- safety of an Oracle policy or of the maximum registered action without a certificate;
- query bootstrap as an unseen-build confidence interval.

## Evidence language

Use “registered-family evidence,” “fixed-target certificate,” “query-law uncertainty conditional on registered builds,” and “Vamana-style implementation.” Avoid “universal,” “open-world recovery,” “guaranteed future build,” and “safe fallback” unless the fallback has independent evidence under the same target event.
