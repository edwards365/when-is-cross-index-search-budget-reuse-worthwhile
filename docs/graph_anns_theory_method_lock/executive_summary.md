# Graph-ANNS Theory–Method–Claim Final Lock

## Final decision

`THEORY_METHOD_CLAIM_FINAL_LOCK_PASS`

The project now supports a coherent A-conference paper centered on a theory-guided negative result and cross-system measurement study, with ICBA as a certified diagnostic method. It does not support a state-of-the-art retrieval algorithm or open-world positive recovery claim.

## What is locked

The theoretical core is reduced to two main theorems. Main Theorem I applies classical two-point testing to hidden build environments: when observable transcripts do not distinguish environments with conflicting useful actions, every environment-blind policy incurs unsafe under-budgeting, conservative computation, fallback, or abstention in at least one environment. Main Theorem II gives necessary obstructions and a sufficient fixed-target, finite-action recovery protocol based on endpoint feasibility, identifiability, positive risk margin, independent target evidence, and a valid fallback or abstention. It is not an iff characterization.

The empirical core contains six registered data × operator cells: SIFT-100K and Arxiv-Nomic-100K under hnswlib HNSW, Faiss HNSW, and DiskANN3/Vamana-style construction. Budget-category variation ranges from 48.27% to 89.47%. Source-to-target transport-violation effects are positive in every cell, with original endpoint-aware increments from 11.37% to 21.57% and harmonized jointly-feasible sensitivity values from 11.11% to 21.85%. Conservative NDC cost is resolved for both HNSW implementations but not for Vamana-style search, so the paper claims operator-dependent cost consequences rather than a universal tax.

Four method families form a falsification ladder rather than a success leaderboard: recalibration shows that fixed-target safety can be recovered without stable incremental value; build selection and stable construction fail when the candidate pool or structural bridge is inadequate; multi-lane rescue establishes complementarity but lacks a low-cost observable trigger; and the certified auditor avoids unsafe acceptance but often falls back or abstains without decision value.
