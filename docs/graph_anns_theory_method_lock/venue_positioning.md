# Venue positioning

## Recommended order

1. **SIGMOD / PVLDB** — strongest fit. The paper identifies a deployment assumption, provides reproducible cross-system measurement, formalizes the failure boundary, and contributes an auditing method. The lack of a faster search algorithm is acceptable if the systems implication, artifact, and decision protocol are foregrounded.
2. **ICDE** — strong fit for index robustness, evaluation methodology, and operational decision support; slightly lower bar for theory novelty than a theory-first ML venue.
3. **ICLR / NeurIPS** — plausible but higher risk. The submission must emphasize hidden algorithmic environments, non-portability, and the general form of reconfiguration risk, while being transparent that the main inequalities are classical and empirical scope is Graph-ANNS.

| Dimension | DB systems venues | ICLR/NeurIPS |

| Empirical breadth | high | medium |

| General theorem novelty | medium | medium-low |

The recommended framing is a negative-result-plus-measurement paper with a certified auditing methodology. A claim of SOTA search, open-world recovery, or a new general statistical theory would weaken rather than strengthen the submission.

## Readiness

Theory depth is assessed at 7.5/10 for a systems paper and 6/10 for a theory-first ML paper. Overall paper readiness is 88%: the scientific core is locked, but the manuscript still needs polished figures, a concise related-work synthesis with primary-source citations, and a proof-to-main-text editing pass.
