Conservative claim boundary: the reviewed literature already establishes query heterogeneity, index-aware hardness, adaptive search, and insertion-order effects. The remaining empirical contribution is the measured non-portability of the same queries' stable budgets across controlled HNSW construction histories, together with the Price of Index Blindness and finite-sentinel rebuild certification cost. This is not a claim of being the first work to connect query hardness and index structure.
