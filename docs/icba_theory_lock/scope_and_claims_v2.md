# Locked scope and claims

## Mathematical scope

ICBA applies to budgeted decision systems with ordered actions, measurable information classes, defined quality/cost, and explicit pointwise or marginal risk. Main Result I is mechanism-general. Main Result II's barrier identity is general for discrete query-specific monotone costs, while its inversion matching proposition is narrow. Main Result III requires frozen policies and independent calibration. Main Result IV requires acquisition cost accounting; Bellman recursion additionally needs finite Markov/known-transition assumptions.

## Empirical scope

Direct evidence remains the frozen train-side Graph-ANNS matrix: hnswlib, Faiss HNSW, and Vamana on SIFT-100K, GloVe-100K, and Arxiv-Nomic-100K. Strong actionable evidence remains hnswlib-specific. Trace Oracles cover SIFT seed7 and GloVe seeds7/17/29 and are non-deployable. No validation-dev or formal-test result exists.

## Strongest allowed claim

To our knowledge under the reviewed literature scope, ICBA is the first integrated formulation that characterizes lossless rebuilt-index budget portability by source-summary measurability, decomposes safe monotone tax into exact budget barriers, incorporates random fail-closed certification cost, and identifies cost-adjusted target-native information as the method escape route, with frozen contact across three Graph-ANNS implementations.

## Forbidden claims

- universal practical rebuild tax across ANN methods;
- nine positive average taxes equal nine independently confirmed history effects;
- cross-implementation NDC equivalence;
- T4 as a general nonlinear-cost theorem;
- GloVe 0/16 as mainly a multiplicity failure;
- high AUC as a certificate;
- perfect-label trace Oracle as algorithm performance;
- more information necessarily lowers total operational cost;
- any validation-dev or formal-test generalization.
