# ICBA four-result novelty re-audit

This is a targeted re-audit of the four locked results, using the 12 primary sources already verified in the preceding sprint. It is not a systematic review. No unverified citation enters the formal bibliography.

## Main Result I

Blackwell comparison of experiments supplies the generic fact that richer information weakly improves attainable decision value. Robust/conditional decision reasoning supplies the essential-supremum intuition. ICBA does not claim either generic principle. The contribution is the rebuild-conditioned ANN budget specialization, the exact zero-tax measurability characterization under strict cost, and frozen 648-pair contact. Safest claim: “To our knowledge under the reviewed scope, this source-summary envelope has not been used to characterize portable per-query budgets across rebuilt ANN indexes.”

## Main Result II

Order-restricted inference and ranking theory are mathematical neighbors. ICBA's exact telescoping barrier identity is elementary rather than a new general mathematical discovery; its value is the budget-level decomposition and empirical instrumentation. The matching bound is retained only as a restricted proposition. Safest claim: the paper introduces an ICBA-specific barrier accounting that separates information aliasing, monotonicity, barrier mass, and cost jumps. Forbidden claim: a general new inversion-regret theorem.

## Main Result III

Conformal Risk Control, Learn-then-Test, and exact binomial inference already establish finite-sample risk certification and multiplicity effects. ICBA inherits these tools. The contribution is the fail-closed deployment value linking certification probability, fallback, calibration amortization, and ANN work, plus the frozen GloVe failure-count autopsy. Forbidden claim: invention of conformal/PAC certification or the general ranking–calibration distinction.

## Main Result IV

Optimal stopping and filtration-based decision theory are established. ICBA's contribution is the cost-adjusted interface for target-native resumable ANN state, separating statistical information value from prefix acquisition/repetition cost and connecting it to frozen trace Oracles. Forbidden claim: a new Bellman principle or an implemented optimal stopping method.

## Overall positioning

The defensible novelty is a coherent specialization and empirical bridge: rebuilt index instance → conditional query demand → source-information aliasing → exact monotone barrier cost → marginal-risk relaxation → random finite-sample certification → cost-adjusted target-state escape. Standard component theorems are credited as inherited where applicable. The project may claim a new problem formulation and integrated boundary analysis only with “to our knowledge, under the reviewed literature scope.”
