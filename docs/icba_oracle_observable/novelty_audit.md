# Novelty audit

## Controlling conclusion

No result in this closure justifies claiming a new Le Cam, Blackwell, Fano, Clopper-Pearson, uniform-convergence, reject-option, or cost-sensitive-classification technique. The viable novelty is the problem construction and restricted combination: hidden rebuild environment, ordered under-budget safety, full evidence/fallback cost, oracle-observable gap, and a split-certified recovery template.

The project does not supersede any of them.

## Method-neighbor boundary

ANNiE, QBAT, ConANN, DARTH+, Ada-ef, and related ANNS budget controllers are relevant method competitors because they adapt per-query effort or target recall/cost. The two attached ANNiE/QBAT PDFs were not present in the current scratch at final audit time, so this closure does not upgrade them to theorem-level full-text findings. No “first rebuild-portable ANNS” language is permitted until fixed-index assumptions, per-rebuild profiling, target labels, and rebuild transfer are verified from accessible full text.

## Independent candidate, narrowly stated

The strongest defensible candidate is not an isolated inequality. It is the paired statement:

> In a finite ordered-budget recovery problem with hard safety and explicit fallback/evidence cost, indistinguishable target transcripts impose a decision-loss floor, while a valid margin-separated target channel plus independent selected-action certification yields a fixed-target near-oracle recovery rule.

Even this is a `NEW_COMBINATION_OF_CLASSICAL_RESULTS`, not `ORIGINAL_CORE_CANDIDATE`, until a nontrivial joint rate or frontier exceeds modular composition.
