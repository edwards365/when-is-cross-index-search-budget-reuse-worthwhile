# BN-APD observable-trigger instrumentation gate

## Outcome

`OBSERVABLE_TRIGGER_NOT_SUPPORTED_CLOSE_BN_APD_ROUTE`

The project-side instrumentation was valid, but the preregistered deployment-visible trigger family did not recover the oracle marginal portal opportunity. Consequently, the shared-frontier smoke is not authorized.

## Evidence

- 12,000 truth-free trace rows: two datasets, three builds per dataset, 500 replay-development queries, and four fixed ef values.
- Native/tracer top-10 mismatches: 0.
- Eight preregistered rules were assessed: three kth-distance stagnation windows, three low-candidate-yield thresholds, proposal-frozen high-duplication, and proposal-frozen high-frontier-ratio.
- Rules passing both datasets: 0/8.
- The highest validation median advantage after deleting the top 1% advantage remained 0.0. Small positive means appeared only in sparse cells below the 10% coverage gate; they are not qualifying evidence.
- Coverage-qualified cells did not provide a strictly positive median portal-over-continuation value across at least two of three builds in both datasets.
- The historical 500-query pool was explicitly treated as `bn_apd_replay_development`; this is not independent confirmation.

## Interpretation

The previous portal-rescue oracle effect remains a valid retrospective observation, and the candidate-union calculation remains a cost lower bound. This stage shows that the fixed, inexpensive observable states tested here do not identify that value robustly enough for deployment. It does not prove that no trigger could ever exist, but it closes the current BN-APD route under the preregistered action and feature class. No graph mutation, new portal, shared-frontier implementation, model race, sealed query role, or truth-derived trigger feature was used.
