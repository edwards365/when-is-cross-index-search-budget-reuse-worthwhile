# Final recommendation

## Recommendation

Proceed only to the frozen two-dataset pilot for **Stabilize-then-Certify with a Critical-Path/Frontier repair core**. Retain **Consensus-Stabilized Construction with protected bridges** as the sole fallback construction route. Keep canonical deterministic construction as a baseline and Best-of-R as a costed comparator.

The theoretical case is positive but narrow. T-SC2 supplies the risk interface; T-SC5/T-SC10 supply a restricted structure-to-budget certificate; classical LTT/RCPS/binomial tools supply fixed-target certification; T-SC8 supplies the service Gate. The general structural surrogate is false, and no open-world positive recovery theorem has been established.

## Why not a stronger label

The complete chain holds only for registered deterministic traces with retained introductions, fixed priority semantics and bounded frontier intrusion. Those assumptions can be probed on a workload but not certified uniformly over arbitrary future queries/builds. The statistical pieces are classical and the search-path/pruning literature materially overlaps the proof idea. Therefore the work is best positioned as a database/systems method proposal with supporting restricted theory, not a NeurIPS general-theory contribution.

## Next decision

If the pilot meets all ten Gates, implement a production-quality degree-constrained repair and expand to one external implementation at realistic scale. If structure improves but `d_B` does not, stop and report `STRUCTURAL_SURROGATE_LINK_UNPROVED`. If `d_B` improves but certification/cost does not, retain the empirical stability result but do not claim deployment value. If no finite break-even exists, fall back to canonical build or per-target retraining/recalibration.

Final label: `STABILIZE_THEN_CERTIFY_TEMPLATE_AUTHORIZED`.
