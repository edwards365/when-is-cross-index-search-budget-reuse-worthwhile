# Claim ledger

## Allowed now

- Build environments can change Graph-ANNS query-budget response; direct evidence exists for insertion-order recall/cost variation.
- Construction reproducibility, edge similarity, path similarity, budget-response stability and end-to-end cost are different objects.
- Generic edge overlap does not control minimum safe budget.
- Under an upward-closed recall event and a one-sided budget coupling, a grid-shifted source policy has target under-budget risk at most source risk plus coupling failure.
- Under a robust deterministic trace certificate with bounded frontier interference, retained critical structure controls a one-sided budget shift.
- Stable construction can reduce a *classical fixed-target* certification burden when it creates a positive effective safety margin.
- A Graph-ANNS-specific Stabilize-then-Certify template and build/service break-even condition are defined.
- The proposed pilot is falsifiable and may fail because of recall, tail latency, build cost, endpoint infeasibility or non-transferable traces.

## Allowed only with narrow scope

- “Critical-path stabilization controls migration risk” — only in the T-SC5/T-SC10 model, not arbitrary HNSW.
- “Structural surrogate” — only the registered trace/frontier certificate on the measured query scope.
- “Lowers certification sample size” — only after a validated risk-shift bound leaves `gamma_eff > 0`.
- “Open-world potential” — motivation only; no open-world guarantee.

## Forbidden

- first stable Graph-ANNS construction method;
- first study of HNSW insertion-order sensitivity;
- new generic Le Cam, KL, PAC, DKW or concentration theorem;
- edge overlap necessarily controls recall, budget or safety;
- canonical ordering solves open-world migration;
- the method works across arbitrary Graph-ANNS implementations;
- real mean or p95 latency is already improved;
- target evidence is unnecessary;
- the template is a validated deployment algorithm;
- NeurIPS-level general theory is closed;
- dynamic updates are solved;
- general-graph matching upper and lower bounds exist;
- validation-dev or formal-test confirms any result.
