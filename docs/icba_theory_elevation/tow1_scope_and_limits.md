# T-OW1 scope, novelty and limits

The standard component is Le Cam's two-point indistinguishability/coupling step. The added modeling content is not TV itself: it identifies a one-sided failure mode on an ordered budget lattice, keeps conservative over-allocation and fallback distinct from unsafe under-allocation, identifies which target-information channel changes the testing distance, and prevents query-level evidence from being promoted to build-level reliability.

The theorem is general beyond Graph-ANNS whenever environments share the stated action conflict. The empirical Graph-ANNS data supplies witnesses and motivates the channels; it does not prove that every graph index family satisfies the premises.

Target probes help only through the law they observe. `k` can shrink testing error for in-support environments while leaving response misspecification untouched. More historical builds `m` can estimate a meta-risk only under a build-sampling law; more queries `n` refine within-build risk only. No sample size repairs absent support without an additional structural assumption.

The theorem does not prove a deployable algorithm, a universal linear cost tradeoff, or safety when endpoints do not exist. ECSE remains a closed-world construction with labeled sentinels and an Oracle base policy, not a matching arbitrary-open-world solution.
