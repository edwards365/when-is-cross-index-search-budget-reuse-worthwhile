# Claim registry

## Allowed now

- The ICBA finite-environment, finite-native-action framework can be instantiated for Vamana.
- HNSW and Vamana require different build, budget and cost semantics.
- Cross-family replication does not require equal numerical effect sizes.
- Data-by-operator interaction is a legitimate preregistered target.
- An asymmetric held-out-target design avoids the complete-pair symmetry artifact.
- The frozen literature search found no direct nine-condition precedent within reviewed scope.

## Conditional on a passed pilot

- Registered Vamana rebuilds exhibit safe-budget response heterogeneity.
- The phenomenon has a material one-sided risk or within-implementation conservative-cost consequence.
- A two-dataset pass supports registered DiskANN3/Vamana family-level replication.

## Forbidden

- Vamana already has a budget-transport problem.
- All Graph-ANNS implementations have the same problem.
- `efSearch` is equivalent to Vamana `l_value`.
- Structural graph change entails budget-response change.
- RobustPrune increases or decreases stability in a fixed direction.
- The work proves open-world impossibility or Vamana recovery.
- Existing results establish Graph-ANNS universality.
- This is the first rebuild-portability study.
- The largest registered action is automatically safe.
- Raw recall is monotone in native budget.
