# Directed-pair symmetry note

For complete reverse pairs and jointly feasible raw minimal budgets, under- and over-budget direction counts are algebraically equal. This makes a symmetric full-pair H2 largely a decomposition of H1 rather than an independent phenomenon.

The main pilot therefore uses six preregistered source builds and six disjoint held-out target builds per dataset. It estimates only the 36 source-to-target directions. The reverse target-to-source directions may be tabulated later as an explicitly descriptive analysis and may not enter the primary test, thresholds or tie-breaking.

The identity does not apply to censored budgets, missing reverse pairs, unequal direction weights, policy-derived actions that are not minimal safe budgets, or claims about actual transferred-action safety under nonmonotone response. Censored mass and raw nonmonotonicity are reported separately.
