# Supplement Loop 3: SIGMOD E&A paper-facing compliance seal

Objective: produce a concise, machine-auditable paper evidence map under the
SIGMOD 2027 E&A rules. This loop changes no experiment and does not rewrite the
existing manuscript automatically.

Required checks:

1. proposed title ends in `: [Experiments & Analysis]`;
2. main-body budget is at most 12 pages excluding references;
3. the primary contribution is new insight into rebuild portability and method
   strengths/weaknesses, not a universal new-method or SOTA claim;
4. every headline number maps to a committed table, manifest, script, test, and
   claim boundary;
5. TCP is stated as target-selection recalibration with amortized
   native-distance value at N=1e6, not as raw source transfer or controlled
   wall-clock superiority;
6. Vamana remains labeled post-hoc semantic alignment;
7. Deep1M remains first-1M/eight-build evidence;
8. the anonymous artifact link placeholder and required reproducibility text
   are present, with no identity-bearing path;
9. limitations include the one non-amortizing Arxiv target build and external
   data/index setup.

Outputs: a 12-page section budget, claim-to-paper table, headline-number
ledger, reviewer-risk checklist, recommended E&A title/abstract/contribution
language, and an updated route score. Pass requires no unsupported headline
claim and no known desk-rejection-format violation in the proposed package.
