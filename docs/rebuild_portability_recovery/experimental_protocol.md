# Experimental protocol

Primary scope is hnswlib on SIFT-100K and Arxiv-Nomic-100K, nine frozen builds per dataset and 144 directed source–target pairs. Same-history cross-seed and leave-one-history-out are reported separately. Build is the outer independent unit; 500 sentinel repetitions are not treated as independent builds. Safety precedes NDC value, then robustness, complexity and online overhead. Gate V1 requires 50% less target truth or lower refusal than M1; V2 requires at least 1% lower total cost than full retraining at a preregistered workload; V3 requires safe conversion of unsafe accepts to fallback. Truth cost that is unavailable is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, never zero. All results are design-stage only; validation-dev/formal-test, new graph construction, GPU, complex model search and confirmatory labels are forbidden.
