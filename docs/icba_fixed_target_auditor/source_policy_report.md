# Source policy report

Six minimal `SOURCE_GLOBAL_FIXED_EF` policies were serialized for the original hnswlib builds of SIFT and Arxiv. They use only historical source-development outcomes on the common six-level grid and select minimum mean NDC subject to historical source risk at most 0.05. All six select ef=120. These policies are deployable action definitions, but their historical source risks are construction diagnostics rather than target safety certificates.
