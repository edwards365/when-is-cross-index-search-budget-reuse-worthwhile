# Claim ledger

## Allowed with current scope

- Graph-ANNS builds can be treated as finite deployment actions together with raw fixed-`ef` budgets.
- Shared target truth can evaluate multiple preregistered build-budget actions while retaining action-specific search costs.
- A fixed candidate family admits finite-sample simultaneous safe selection through exact-binomial Bonferroni bounds.
- Arbitrary data-dependent mean-cost selection inside the simultaneously certified set preserves the risk certificate.
- Endpoint infeasibility and right-censoring must enter the failure/abstention semantics.
- Build, truth, candidate-search, selection, and certification costs yield an explicit service break-even calculation.
- Paired full-information comparisons may reduce cost-estimation variance when covariance is positive.
- The method emits one build, one raw global fixed-`ef`, one certificate, and one fallback.

Every empirical benefit claim must be prefixed by `EXPLORATORY_OFFLINE_SIMULATION` until the pilot measures true NDC and tails.

## Forbidden

- first safe index selection method;
- new generic SafeBAI, Track-and-Stop, active testing, or KL lower-bound theory;
- first safe budget certification;
- first rebuild-portable ANNS;
- unconditional open-world recovery or next-build safety;
- candidate count necessarily improves benefit, or (K=2) necessarily beats (K=3);
- shared truth makes multi-build evaluation free;
- query-level certification equals build-level/portfolio-level certification;
- budget-proxy improvement equals NDC, p95, or deployment gain;
- CIBS needs no target evidence;
- CIBS is already profitable, tail-improving, implementation-general, or NeurIPS-theory novel.

## Claims pending pilot evidence

- positive real mean-NDC gain;
- p95/p99 non-inferiority;
- finite build-service break-even;
- robustness to deletion of the top 1% gains;
- absence of single-build dominance;
- reproducibility across independent portfolios;
- applicability beyond hnswlib/SIFT-100K/Arxiv-Nomic-100K.
