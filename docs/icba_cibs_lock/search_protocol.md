# Directed full-text search protocol

## Frozen scope

The review targeted 10–15 highest-risk neighbors rather than expanding the bibliography. A paper was Level A only when its full text, theorem statements, assumptions, and relevant proof or proof route were read. Abstracts, programs, snippets, or secondary descriptions were insufficient to clear a direct-prior risk.

## Direct-prior test

A direct CIBS predecessor must jointly provide:

1. multiple candidate Graph-ANNS builds on one dataset;
2. build and search budget as a joint deployment action;
3. shared target queries or exact truth;
4. simultaneous control of recall-failure risk;
5. low-cost selection inside the safe set;
6. multi-build and certification cost accounting; and
7. validation across independent portfolios/build units.

The absence of one item yields at most theorem/module overlap, not a direct method precedent.

## Read-status rules

- `LEVEL_A_FULLTEXT_PROOF_CHECKED`: full text and relevant proofs checked.
- `LEVEL_B_FULLTEXT_PARTIAL`: full text available but proof coverage incomplete.
- `LEVEL_C_PUBLIC_FRAGMENT`: abstract, introduction, program, or fragment only.
- `FULLTEXT_UNVERIFIED`: full text not obtained in this round.

## Level-A corpus checked this round

SafeBAI; Track-and-Stop; LUCB/PAC subset selection; Learn-Then-Test; RCPS; Conformal Risk Control; Hyperband; SATzilla; time-uniform Chernoff bounds; correlated-arm BAI; ANNiE; and ConANN. QBAT was retained as `FULLTEXT_UNVERIFIED` for this round because only the official VLDB program record and abstract were freshly available.

## Search discipline

Forward/backward searches were restricted to safe/constrained BAI, full-information or correlated-arm selection, algorithm portfolios/racing, post-selection risk control, confidence sequences, and ANN query budgeting. Broadly related database tuning papers were not allowed to clear or threaten the narrow seven-condition claim without theorem-level overlap.

## Reproducibility and access limits

The designated server worktree was not mounted in this environment, so server process and server-side sealed-set status were not claimed as directly observed. The audit used an isolated staging directory and repository snapshots at the stated commits. No validation-dev, formal-test, sentinel truth, evaluation truth, or future-confirm truth was opened.
