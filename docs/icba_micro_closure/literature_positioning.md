# Targeted Literature Positioning

This is a bounded positioning audit, not a systematic review. It reuses the primary-source families already verified in the preceding ICBA sprint and the preregistered named directions; no claim of absolute priority is made.

Adaptive ANNS systems such as learned early termination/AdaptNN, Tao, DARTH and Ada-ef primarily optimize query effort for a fixed or trained-on index regime. Work on HNSW insertion order and Graph-ANNS worst cases establishes that construction and topology affect search, but does not by itself provide a safe source-policy transfer rule after an index is replaced. ICBA's narrow question is the combination: an old query-budget policy, a replaced computational environment, limited target sentinels, a marginal query-risk constraint and fully charged adaptation cost.

Conformal Risk Control, Learn-Then-Test and risk-controlled early exit supply finite-sample marginal certification tools; conditional-coverage impossibility results explain why arbitrary per-cell safety cannot be obtained distribution-free. ECSE inherits rather than claims these ideas. Its contribution in this sprint is a finite closed-world environment confidence set coupled to a safe budget envelope and a fail-closed endpoint, with sentinel acquisition cost separated from query execution.

Domain-adaptation impossibility, multi-environment predictive inference and hierarchical distribution-free inference emphasize that target information and environment sampling assumptions determine what can transfer. Learning-augmented algorithms, prediction portfolios, conditional robust optimization, side-information uncertainty sets and Blackwell informativeness provide neighboring decision principles. The overlap--separation bound is therefore presented as a restricted aligned-response theorem, not a general replacement for those literatures.

The Graph-ANNS replay sharpens the remaining gap: exact closed-world identification can recover an Oracle-like envelope, but nearest retained environments do not safely dominate a left-out build. The current method is consequently closed-world only and nondeployable because the frozen trace permits labeled target sentinels plus per-query source Oracle responses, not an operational source policy.

The defensible statement is:

> To the best of our targeted review, existing work studies subsets of fixed-environment adaptive computation, risk control, multi-environment inference, and learning-augmented robustness, while the joint problem of safe budget-policy portability under computational environment replacement remains underexplored.

No “first” claim is authorized. A future ICBA v2 paper would need a systematic search, exact bibliography verification and a genuinely open-world or hierarchical-environment upper bound.
