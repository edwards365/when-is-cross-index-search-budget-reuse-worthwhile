# Targeted literature audit

This route audit rechecked ten already-frozen Level-A full texts; it did not claim ten newly downloaded papers. The set was deliberately narrow: Track-and-Stop, SafeBAI, Learn then Test, distribution-free RCPS, ANNiE, QBAT, DARTH, FreshDiskANN, Yang et al. on proximity-graph construction, and MARGO.

## CIBS

The statistical ingredients are classical and crowded. Track-and-Stop and SafeBAI cover fixed-confidence identification; LTT/RCPS directly expose the multiple-testing defect in per-action CP selection. None of the checked papers fully specifies paired full-information selection over serialized Graph-ANNS build×ordered-budget actions with endpoint censoring, amortized build costs, and both query- and build-level uncertainty. CIBS therefore requires a narrow redefinition: the novelty cannot be “safe selection” alone.

## CRSF

ANNiE, QBAT and DARTH create a high direct-prior threat for learned per-query effort and termination. Continue-not-restart plus independent target certification is a defensible difference, but frozen realistic headroom is not yet two-dataset positive.

## RPA

FreshDiskANN demonstrates real index maintenance, while generic monitor/retrain work threatens the broad story. The current action space is not serialized, so novelty cannot rescue deployability.

## DBRSC

Yang et al. and MARGO strongly overlap path-aware construction objectives. A claim must center on a direct budget-response objective under hard Recall/risk constraints; no frozen finite optimizer currently exists.

## Conclusion

No single checked Level-A paper completely covers CIBS's narrow joint object, but its safety component must explicitly use simultaneous testing. CRSF and DBRSC face stronger systems/construction collisions; RPA is presently blocked before novelty becomes decisive.
