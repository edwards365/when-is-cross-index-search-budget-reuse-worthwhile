# Pareto dominance report

Risk UCB, mean/p95 NDC and labels are compared for B0, B1/B2 max, RM-B1/RM-B2 stages, randomized mixtures, ASRC and B5 proxy. ASRC is not granted an independent increment: its same-risk matched RM improvements fail the 10%/5% requirements and confidence lower-bound criteria. Any apparent B1-max advantage is not sufficient evidence.
