# Paper positioning

## Story A — recommended
Graph-ANNS budget certificates are not portable to unseen rebuilds without environment-relatedness assumptions. This negative theory plus the three-implementation empirical boundary is the strongest current story.

## Story B — deferred
A lower-bound/recovery/fallback loop is not supported because Z0 fails and Z1 is unavailable. Structured recovery requires new preregistered observables and independent builds in a later phase.

## Story C — viable application framing
Construction history is a query-budget condition variable ignored by fixed-index adaptive ANN evaluations. This remains suitable for an ANN systems paper even if the broad theory is repositioned.
