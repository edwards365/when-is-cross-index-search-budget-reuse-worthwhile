# Limitations and non-claims

- The evidence is stratified by implementation and dataset; it is not a
  universal theorem about every Graph-ANNS rebuild.
- DARTH and Ada-ef retain raw efficiency value but fail the registered transfer
  certificate. Their audited fixed-safe deployments have zero gain here; this
  is not a claim that the methods are generally ineffective.
- Vamana evidence is a post-hoc semantic realignment of frozen outputs. Its
  portability-risk result is quantitative, but the efficiency interval crosses
  zero.
- Deep1M uses the first one million deep-image vectors and eight registered
  hnswlib builds. Outer-build uncertainty remains finite-sample evidence.
- TCP's positive result is target-selection recalibration under a mixed 5%
  refresh. Raw source reuse is unsafe. Search-distance break-even is measured,
  while truth, rebuild, and control costs are not harmonized; full-lifecycle
  economic superiority is therefore not estimable.
- No result in this artifact supports absolute SOTA or open-world deployment.
