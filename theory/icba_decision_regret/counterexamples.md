# Counterexamples

For actions a,b and environments 0,1, two classifiers can each be 50% accurate: one makes its error where the cost gap is 1, the other where it is 100; regrets differ by 100x. In this experiment, replaying uncertified proposals appeared strongly positive, while enforcing fixed-safe fallback made SIFT gain negative and worsened pooled p95 on both datasets. Thus proposal accuracy and pre-certificate cost are not deployment value.
