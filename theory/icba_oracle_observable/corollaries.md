# Corollaries

1. If `P_0^m=P_1^m`, T-OO3 gives maximum decision loss at least `Delta/2`; T-OO2 attains the hard-safety fallback form.
2. If `I*=0`, no finite or infinite sequence of the allowed probes distinguishes the pair; increasing sentinel count cannot close the oracle–observable gap.
3. If `TV(P_0^m,P_1^m)=1`, this testing obstruction vanishes, but safety margins, support, endpoint feasibility, and evidence cost can still block recovery.
4. Query-level certification for one target says nothing about a new build unless an explicit exchangeable build model and build-level calibration sample are added.
5. Under T-OO5's event, RACS is safe and `2 epsilon_C`-near-oracle before certification overhead and fallback probability are added.
6. Zero failures certify 10%, 5%, and 1% risks at 95% confidence with at least 29, 59, and 299 iid units, respectively; the relevant unit must match the risk being certified.
