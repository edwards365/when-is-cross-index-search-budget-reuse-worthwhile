# Open problems

1. Obtain a non-circular deployable target observation channel with empirically positive decision-relevant information.
2. Match T-OO8 beyond the known binary finite-alphabet channel, including adaptive heterogeneous sentinel choice and ordered actions.
3. Derive a joint `m historical builds, n within-build queries, k target probes, M actions` complexity rather than a union of separate bounds.
4. Characterize the minimax safety/search/truth/fallback frontier under hard constraints.
5. Extend fixed-target RACS to a valid meta-environment guarantee with uncertain within-build risk labels.
6. Handle right-censored safe budgets without treating the maximum observed budget as safe.
7. Determine when ordered budgets reduce identification complexity relative to an unstructured action family.
