# Reading cards: spectral algorithms and localization

Each card records: object, assumptions, theorem, proof mechanism, complexity, relation to HNSW, transferability, missing conditions, verification level, and project action.

## Spielman--Srivastava: Graph Sparsification by Effective Resistances

1. **Object:** connected weighted undirected Laplacian. 2. **Assumptions:** positive conductances and independent samples. 3. **Theorem:** Theorem 1 gives a spectral sparsifier; Lemma 3 identifies the cut-space projector. 4. **Mechanism:** matrix concentration applied to resistance-normalized edge rank-one terms. 5. **Complexity:** nearly-linear approximation route in the paper, distinct from our dense oracle. 6. **HNSW relation:** supplies leverage semantics only on a declared symmetrization. 7. **Transfer:** projector and rank-one identities transfer. 8. **Missing:** independent sampling and absence of degree/reverse-pruning coupling. 9. **Verified:** full primary PDF. 10. **Action:** cite Lemma 3; never call deterministic local repair a spectral sparsifier without checking the matrix inequality.

## Durfee et al.: Sampling Random Spanning Trees Faster than Matrix Multiplication

1. **Object:** weighted random spanning trees, Schur complements, approximate resistances. 2. **Assumptions:** connected undirected weighted graph and paper error/failure parameters. 3. **Theorem:** Theorems 2.1--2.3 give sampling/ApproxSchur/resistance routines; Fact 5.4 gives exact terminal-resistance preservation. 4. **Mechanism:** recursive Schur complementation and approximate Laplacian primitives. 5. **Complexity:** paper-specific near-linear/subquadratic bounds, not reproduced as an implementation claim here. 6. **HNSW relation:** identifies the correct exact localized terminal graph. 7. **Transfer:** Fact 5.4 transfers exactly. 8. **Missing:** a scalable terminal-set selection and approximation-error/rank-margin audit. 9. **Verified:** full primary PDF. 10. **Action:** compare induced one/two-hop graphs with exact global Kron reductions on small instances.

## Sugiyama--Sato: Kron Reduction and Effective Resistance of Directed Graphs

1. **Object:** directed Laplacians, directed Kron reduction, asymmetric resistance. 2. **Assumptions:** reachability/strong connectivity and, for some results, weight balance. 3. **Theorem:** official abstract states preservation of strong connectivity, weight balance, and the paper's resistance. 4. **Mechanism:** Schur complements of directed Laplacians. 5. **Complexity:** not audited. 6. **HNSW relation:** potentially relevant to directed adjacency. 7. **Transfer:** none claimed yet. 8. **Missing:** full theorem labels, exact admissible terminal sets, and a reason to prefer this directed notion over the project's symmetrized score. 9. **Verified:** official abstract only. 10. **Action:** keep `imported_unverified`; do not mix its resistance with ordinary electrical resistance.

## Spielman--Teng: Spectral Sparsification of Graphs

1. **Object:** undirected graph spectral approximation. 2. **Assumptions:** weighted Laplacian setting and paper sampling/solver model. 3. **Theorem:** existence/algorithmic sparsification results. 4. **Mechanism:** spectral graph decomposition and sampling. 5. **Complexity:** nearly-linear family of bounds in the paper. 6. **HNSW relation:** conceptual baseline for preserving quadratic forms. 7. **Transfer:** only the definition of spectral approximation. 8. **Missing:** adjacency-navigation stability, degree caps, and directedness. 9. **Verified:** primary arXiv PDF metadata/abstract plus source-map boundary; exact theorem labels not used. 10. **Action:** use Spielman--Srivastava for the claims actually invoked.

## Yang--Tang: Efficient Estimation of Pairwise Effective Resistance

1. **Object:** pairwise ER queries on large undirected unweighted graphs. 2. **Assumptions:** the paper's random-walk representation and epsilon/failure parameters. 3. **Theorem:** Theorem 3.1 bounds walk truncation; Theorem 3.4 proves Algorithm 1's approximation, and Section 3.3.2 gives complexity. 4. **Mechanism:** truncated random walks, Hoeffding/empirical Bernstein bounds, and adaptive sampling; GEER mixes sampling with sparse matrix-vector multiplication. 5. **Complexity:** the exact parameterized bounds in Table 1/Section 3.3.2, not reduced here to an HNSW build claim. 6. **HNSW relation:** possible scalable score estimator. 7. **Transfer:** the ER estimator can transfer after fixing an undirected graph and tolerances. 8. **Missing:** weighted/local extension details, all-candidate amortization, rank-margin preservation, and any navigation bridge. 9. **Verified:** full author arXiv preprint corresponding to DOI 10.1145/3588696. 10. **Action:** benchmark only after dense-oracle score/rank calibration.
